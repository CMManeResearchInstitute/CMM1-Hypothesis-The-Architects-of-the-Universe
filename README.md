### 📌 Project Overview (Introduction)
This repository serves as the central academic and open-science hub for the **CMM1 Hypothesis**, developed at the **C. M. Mane Research Institute**. 

The research framework bridges cosmology, quantum biology, and space microbiology—demonstrating how microbial intelligence acts as the primary architect of planetary habitability, ecological stability, and cosmic evolution.
